Summary of Key Points

Does the App collect personal information? No. The App does not require account creation, does not collect names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information.

Does the App use the Accessibility Service? Yes. The App uses Android's Accessibility API solely to simulate on-screen taps at the positions and intervals you configure. It does not read, record, or transmit the content displayed on your screen.

Are my saved configurations uploaded anywhere? No. Any configuration you save (tap points, interval, time limit) is stored only in your device's local storage and never leaves your device.

Does the App show ads? The App may display third-party advertising (Google AdMob). If enabled, AdMob may collect limited device and advertising identifiers as described in Section 4 below.

1. Use of the Accessibility Service

The App requests Android's Accessibility Service permission for one purpose only: to programmatically simulate taps (via dispatchGesture()) at screen coordinates you choose, at the interval and duration you configure.

Specifically, the Accessibility Service is not used to:

  • Read, log, or transmit the content of your screen or other apps
  • Collect keystrokes, passwords, or any personal data
  • Send any information over the internet

You can review and revoke this permission at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

2. What Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (the App has no accounts or sign-in)
  • Screen content, screenshots, or accessibility event data
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files

3. Local Data Storage

The App lets you save named configurations (tap point positions, click interval, and time limit) so you can reuse them later. This data is stored using Android's local SharedPreferences storage on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared with us or any third party. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

4. Advertising (AdMob)

The App may include Google AdMob to display advertisements. If active, AdMob may automatically collect certain information to serve ads, which can include your advertising ID, general device information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob data disclosure. You can manage ad personalization in your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

If a future version of the App integrates AdMob, this section and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety form will be updated accordingly before that version is published.

7. Data Security

Because saved configurations remain on your device and are never transmitted, there is no server-side data of yours for us to secure or lose. We recommend keeping your device's operating system and this App up to date.
